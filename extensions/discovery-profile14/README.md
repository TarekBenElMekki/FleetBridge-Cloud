# FleetBridge Discovery 1.4.3 — 250-link Google Batches / Analytics Workspace

A light Chrome Manifest V3 extension with two deliberately separate surfaces:

| Capture controls (extension) | Review Workspace (full page) |
| --- | --- |
| Google Search links: a target of **250 distinct LinkedIn candidate URLs** in one click (maximum 40 consecutive pages), or 5/10/20 page modes | Full-width spreadsheet, multi-select filters, sortable candidates and an interactive map |
| Open one selected LinkedIn profile | Name, headline, current location and complete experience text |
| **Capture visible profile**, optionally **Capture extra experience** | Edit each employment period and workplace |
| See response counts and last capture status | Analyze experience, employment categories, workplace-distance distributions and recruitment stages, then review a selected candidate's career and job-location distances |
| **Save & open next** after capture; no lengthy editor or assessment forms | Human **Save review**, status/notes, CSV export, JSON backup/restore |

## How to use

1. In a Google Search results page, open FleetBridge Discovery from Chrome. The batch-size setting defaults to **Target 250 unique candidate links**. Click **Collect candidate links** once: it sequentially captures visible Google result pages, deduplicates LinkedIn profiles across pages, and stops at 250 distinct links, the last page, three consecutive nonproductive pages, a 40-page safety cap, a changed/closed search tab, or a verification screen. It does not bypass access controls or automatically open LinkedIn. Google may provide fewer than 250 eligible results; the final count is reported honestly. Smaller 5/10/20-page modes and **Capture only this page** remain available.
   - The popup shows distinct links, new links, already stored links, repeated links and pages processed. Use **Pause batch** to halt before another page, **Resume batch** to continue from the saved checkpoint, or **Stop run** to end the batch. Pages are spaced 30 seconds apart; collecting 250 can take more than 12 minutes and may end earlier if Google offers no further results. Pause/resume requires the original Google tab to remain available.
2. Click **Profile capture** to open the narrow sidebar. Choose a candidate, click **Open profile for review**, and inspect their profile on LinkedIn in the normal browser tab.
3. Click **Capture visible profile**. It reads currently visible professional information and immediately **stages a local draft**; it does not alter a previously approved review. If more positions exist, manually open LinkedIn's full Experience list, then click **Capture extra experience (optional)**. Neither extraction button navigates on LinkedIn automatically.
4. Click **Save & open next**. The staged capture remains pending human review, and the next queued profile opens in Chrome. Or choose **View results / review data** to switch to the full Workspace.
5. The full-width Workspace displays four aggregate charts (experience, workplace distance, recruitment stage and job categories) and the sortable spreadsheet. Click chart bars to apply multi-select filters; click a candidate in the spreadsheet to jump to the detailed review below. The review shows **four responses**: total experience, latest employer, workplace mobility and France authorization route, as well as a job-category summary, a per-job experience chart and a chart of verified distances between consecutive workplaces. Expand **Professional profile**, **Job timeline**, **Geographic assessment**, **France work authorization checklist** or **Recruitment follow-up** only when needed. Use **Analyze workplaces** and **Assess route** as needed. Click **Save review** to approve data and clear its capture inbox.

The Workspace has an Excel-style full-width table with separate checkbox facets for experience, employment category and workplace-distance bands. An optional **Target role** selector describes directly visible or related profile wording for four vacancy families; it does not determine suitability or automatically shortlist anyone. The real interactive map has thumbtack layers for candidate profile cities and source-backed RATP Group establishments. Aggregate charts distinguish **unverified** data from verified zero distance and are based on the recorded candidate data, not assumed geographic moves. The RATP registry search may not return every operating depot or subsidiary; refresh reports coverage instead of claiming completeness. On a small screen, the charts and review stack without overlays.

## Storage and in-place upgrade

- The old and new releases deliberately keep IndexedDB **`fleetbridge-search-collector`**, object store **`profiles`**, keyed by canonical profile URL. The original Google matches, recruiter status, notes, previously approved `profileImport` fields, and structured job history remain intact.
- New unreviewed evidence is stored separately as **`profileCapture`**. Human-approved records remain in **`profileImport`** until a reviewer explicitly saves a new version. If an already approved profile receives a new capture, use **Use new captured details** in the Workspace to choose whether to replace the approved text; it is not silently overwritten.
- **1.4.2 repairs earlier staged captures**: pending v1.4.1 LinkedIn capture text is re-parsed on Workspace load to correct title/employer fields and reclassify internship/apprenticeship evidence. Existing human-approved `profileImport`, notes, role IDs and recruiter stages are preserved. Misclassified professional headlines in the location field are rejected; the previous text remains available as `rejectedLocation` in the pending-capture record for diagnosis.
- **1.4.3 adds bounded batch checkpoints** in `chrome.storage.local`, separate from candidate IndexedDB. The batch stores at most 250 canonical LinkedIn URLs and progress fields; previous candidate records, review statuses and notes remain unchanged. A stopped or finished batch may be restarted for another search; database duplicates are reported as existing, not discarded. An interrupted active capture is offered as paused after a stale checkpoint, so it does not silently restart navigation.
- **Before upgrading**, export a JSON backup from the old Candidate database. To retain the same extension identity and local database, replace the source files **inside the existing unpacked extension directory**, then click **Reload** for that extension in `chrome://extensions`. Do not remove the old extension or load an unrelated folder unless you intend a new extension identity.
- If a new extension ID is unavoidable, use the existing JSON backup in the new Workspace's **Restore** action. A new extension installation does not automatically share the old one's IndexedDB.
- No backend, Google Sheets API, cloud sync or external FleetBridge main-app integration is required. The Workspace is currently the existing extension's **full-page Candidate database**, accessible in Chrome via its options page. The two surfaces share the same browser-local database.

## Analysis limits

- Experience dates are overlap-aware; concurrent employment counts only once. The table distinguishes **other recorded roles**, **internships** and a separate apprenticeship category, but an unspecified category is *not* proof of paid employment. Category durations can overlap, so summing them can exceed the total. Year-only dates are approximate, and an incomplete history cannot be inferred from hidden or collapsed LinkedIn content. Counts of dated profiles indicate parsed evidence, **not human verification**.
- The distance charts report **straight-line separation between known workplace coordinates**, not actual commute lengths, places of residence, or willingness to move. The overall chart uses the greatest separation for each candidate; the individual chart compares consecutive non-overlapping jobs. Unknown workplaces remain **Unverified**.
- When the Workspace opens, it attempts to resolve reported cities for up to 60 pending staged captures in a pass, using the bundled city reference and, for French communes, the official [French government API](https://geo.api.gouv.fr/). Only **city/location strings** are queried, never names, CVs or profile URLs. It does not silently rewrite previously human-approved jobs. The reviewer's **Analyze workplaces** action remains available for verification or correction. A distance requires at least two separately located job records; a candidate's profile city alone is not enough. French regions are distinguished from departments; reported workplace locations are compared by region code. The city-center straight-line distance is only an approximation.
- A foreign city not in the small bundled reference list remains unresolved until reviewed. Two jobs in different cities **do not prove residential moves**. The tool does not calculate fabricated relocation probabilities; willingness to relocate is explicitly recorded only when confirmed by the candidate.
- The **France work authorization** result is an administrative **checklist**, not a visa decision. A verified permit category, actual French job/region/contract, employer role and applicable current route are needed; special immigration situations require individual assessment. **Autorisation de travail** and an applicable work visa are different procedures. See [Service-Public](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2728) and [France-Visas](https://france-visas.gouv.fr/en/professional-purpose).
- Capture and processing of public profiles can still be restricted by **LinkedIn terms**, including manual browser extensions. Follow site terms and applicable privacy law (including GDPR where relevant); minimize retained personal data, provide required notices and retention controls. The app does not predict age or citizenship or automatically make employment decisions.
- The target-role labels are based solely on visible role/headline keywords and explicitly display what was matched. They are **screening aids**, not evidence of competence, hiring eligibility, permission to work or a recommendation to reject.

## Developer checks

`node --test tests/*.test.cjs` runs self-contained tests for Google/LinkedIn capture, batch deduplication and 250-link stopping, pause/resume, safe legacy capture migration, employer/title corrections, internship breakdowns, role-evidence labels, location validation, staged geocoding, data preservation, Save & Next, chart distributions, genuine map projection, official RATP record normalization, mobility and authorization. The real Chrome UI and current Google/LinkedIn markup still require a manual browser smoke test.
